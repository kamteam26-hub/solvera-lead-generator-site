How scheduling, Gmail intake, and GHL enrollment work

The backend queues enabled leads at four daily California-time slots. A lead is enrolled only once; use a GHL workflow for any later follow-up sequence. Sends happen only on weekdays from 8 AM through 5 PM California local time. Contacts must have valid-format email and US phone plus separately confirmed authenticity and official evidence. Website inquiries are imported into a review queue with outreach disabled; staff must review explicit consent and confirm contact details before enabling GHL. The integration sends email-only payloads—do not add SMS/calls in GHL without separate permission. HTTP success means GHL accepted the webhook, not that later workflow steps completed. Never store patient information or PHI in this CRM or outreach payload.
